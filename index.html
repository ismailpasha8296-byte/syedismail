Baldwin Methodist College is a private institute, established in 1988 in Bangalore, Karnataka. Baldwin Methodist College offers a total of 8 courses at UG levels in 2 specialisations such as English and Physics. Furthermore, it offer courses such as B.Sc., BCA, B.Com, B.A. and BBM/BMS in Full Time mode. It offers courses, in streams such as Science, IT & Software, Business & Management Studies, Accounting & Commerce and Humanities & Social Sciences. The institute has a safe and well-equipped campus with all the advanced facilities.

The college emphasizes holistic development through academic rigor and value-based education. It offers standard facilities including classrooms, libraries, and sports infrastructure, maintaining a disciplined learning environment characteristic of the Baldwin group of institutions.
